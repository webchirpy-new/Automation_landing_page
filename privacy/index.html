3. Your consent

We use the data above because you agree to it when you tick the box on our form. There are two separate choices:

  • Contact about your report and call (needed to send the report or book the call).
  • Tips and updates (optional). Occasional ideas and offers on WhatsApp and email. Saying no does not affect your report or call.

You can withdraw either consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, click unsubscribe in any email, or write to privacy email. We stop within 2 working days. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before you withdrew.

Lawyer to confirm: that contact consent covers follow-up on the enquiry (nurture tracks T1–T5), and that long-term marketing (T7) and community invites need the separate tips-and-updates consent.

5. Cookies and similar tools

We use cookies and similar tools to run the site, measure visits and measure our Meta ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The site will still work, but we will not know which ad brought you.

6. AI tools

We may use AI tools to help draft replies, summaries and reports. We do not put your personal data into any tool that uses it to train public AI models.

To confirm: this is true for every AI tool Webchirpy uses.

7. How long we keep it

  • If you do not become a client: up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then we delete it.
  • If you become a client: for the length of our engagement, then only what tax and accounting law requires us to keep.
  • Proof of your consent: as long as we need it to show that you gave or withdrew consent.
Lawyer to confirm: these retention periods.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with; ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it; withdraw consent; nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act; and raise a grievance with us. Write to privacy email or WhatsApp number. We acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 30 days, and always within the time the law allows.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

We limit access to people who need it, use providers with access controls and encryption, and remove our access to client systems when an engagement ends. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Children

Our service is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If we change how we use your data, we will update this notice and its date. If the change needs new consent, we will ask for it.
